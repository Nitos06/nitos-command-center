# Affiliate Setup — 5-Phase Launch Checklist

Full launch from zero to first affiliate sale. Total time: **3-4 hours setup + 3-5 hours/week ongoing.**

## Quick Reference

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/affiliate-setup phase1` | Pre-install prep checklist |
| `/affiliate-setup phase2` | App configuration guide |
| `/affiliate-setup phase3` | Tracking test suite |
| `/affiliate-setup launch` | Launch day execution plan |
| `/affiliate-setup monitor` | 30-day monitoring schedule |

## Phase 1 — Pre-Install Prep (45-60 min)

- [ ] Calculate net margin per product (spreadsheet: price, COGS, shipping, fees, margin %)
- [ ] Set commission = 30-50% of net margin
- [ ] Choose cookie duration (see table in affiliate-strategy skill)
- [ ] List excluded products (sale items, gift cards, margin < 15%)
- [ ] Write affiliate agreement (12 clauses)
- [ ] Collect 10-15 lifestyle images + 5-10 product-on-white (1080×1080 and 1200×628)
- [ ] Create 3-4 banners (728×90, 300×250, 160×600, 1080×1080)
- [ ] Draft one-page brand guidelines
- [ ] Write 2-3 sample social posts
- [ ] Identify 30-50 initial affiliates:
  - Top 30 repeat customers
  - 10-15 social media taggers
  - 5-10 niche bloggers

## Phase 2 — App Configuration (45-60 min)

- [ ] Install affiliate app (UpPromote or in-house dashboard)
- [ ] Configure base commission rate and exclusions
- [ ] Set $25 minimum payout threshold
- [ ] Set 30-day commission holding period
- [ ] Configure tracking: affiliate links + coupon codes + cookie duration
- [ ] Build registration page (add to footer navigation)
- [ ] Upload marketing materials (images, banners, brand guide)
- [ ] Enable fraud protection (all 4 required settings)
- [ ] Configure payment method and schedule (monthly, 15th)

## Phase 3 — Tracking Tests (15-20 min — NEVER SKIP)

- [ ] Test affiliate link tracking (click → conversion recorded)
- [ ] Test coupon code tracking (coupon → affiliate credited)
- [ ] Test cookie persistence (revisit 48h later, attribution holds)
- [ ] Test attribution priority (link vs. coupon conflict → coupon wins)
- [ ] Test refund handling (commission reversed within 30 days)
- [ ] Test self-referral blocking (own purchase not credited)

## Phase 4 — Launch Day (1-2 hours)

**Morning:**
- [ ] Add "Affiliates" link to footer nav
- [ ] Email top 30 customers (personalize: mention their purchased product)
- [ ] DM 10-15 social taggers (Instagram/TikTok)
- [ ] Post program to affiliate marketplaces

**Afternoon:**
- [ ] Enable post-purchase recruitment banner (show to all buyers)
- [ ] Schedule 24-hour follow-up email for new buyers
- [ ] Enable Day 3 and Day 7 affiliate invite emails

**Target:** 8-15 affiliate signups on launch day.

## Phase 5 — 30-Day Monitoring

**Weekly recurring (15 min):**
- Review dashboard: signups, active affiliates, revenue
- Respond to affiliate questions within 24 hours
- Send onboarding sequence to new signups:
  - Day 0: Welcome + materials
  - Day 3: Content ideas and posting tips
  - Day 7: Check-in + offer help
- Continue recruiting: 20-30 outreach messages/week → target 5-10 new signups/week

**Month-end targets:**
- Affiliate revenue = 3-8% of total store revenue
- Active affiliate ratio = 20-30% of total signups
- Revenue per active affiliate = $100+/month

## First-Month Revenue Ramp

| Week | Signups | Active | Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | 10–18 | 2–5 | $0–$200 |
| Week 2 | 15–25 | 4–8 | $100–$500 |
| Week 3 | 20–35 | 6–12 | $200–$800 |
| Week 4 | 25–50 | 8–15 | $400–$1,500 |

## Zero-Sales Diagnosis

If no sales after 2 weeks:
1. **Commission rate too low?** — Check competitor programs in your niche
2. **Too few signups?** — Need 20+ to get 5-8 active; keep recruiting
3. **Store conversion rate < 1%?** — Fix store first; affiliate traffic won't convert either
4. **Coupon leak?** — Google "brandname + coupon code" to find aggregator sites
